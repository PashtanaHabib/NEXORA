Mission

Ship one accountable team, not three handoffs.

Most software problems aren't technical — they're organizational. Design teams hand specs to engineering teams who hand results back to product, and something gets lost at every seam. We collapse those seams by staffing design and engineering from the same roster on every project.

The result: fewer meetings about alignment, more weeks spent building.
